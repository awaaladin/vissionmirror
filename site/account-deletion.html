Delete your VisionMirror account

You can delete your account, and everything we hold about it, yourself.

In the app

  1. Open VisionMirror and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (or say "Adanna, open settings").
  3. Choose Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.

The account is removed at once. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, name and password hash are permanently deleted from our database.
  • Any conversation held in memory for a recent photo is deleted within 10 minutes, whether or not you had an account.

What stays

Settings and your emergency contact are stored only on your phone, not by us. Uninstalling the app removes them.

If you paid or gave a gift through the website, the record of that payment (email, amount, reference and date) is kept as a financial record, even after the account is deleted. It holds no card details. Write to hiexemedia@gmail.com to ask about it.

Can't open the app?

Write to hiexemedia@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with and ask for deletion.
