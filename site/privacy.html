Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. VisionMirror is a voice assistant for blind and low-vision people, with assistants named Adanna and Bola.

The short version

  • Photos are looked at and then forgotten. They are never saved to a disk or a database.
  • We keep no recordings of your voice.
  • Your location is used only when you ask for it, and is shared only with the person you chose.
  • You can use the app without an account, and delete an account at any time.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera. To take a picture when you ask what is in front of you, to read text, to describe how you look, and for Guide mode. Pictures are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, passed to an AI service to be described, and kept in memory for up to 10 minutes so you can ask follow-up questions. Then they are deleted. Location data in a photo is removed first. Guide mode's object sensing runs entirely on the phone.
  • Microphone. To hear you say the assistant's name and your requests. Listening for the name happens with your phone's own speech recogniser. We do not record or keep your voice. What you ask is turned into text by the phone's speech service (provided by Google on most phones, under its own privacy policy) and the text of your question may be sent to our server to be answered.
  • Location. Only when you ask "where am I" or ask for help. The phone works out where you are. To say the street name aloud, the phone's own address service is used; if it cannot name the place, your approximate position is sent to OpenStreetMap's public address lookup (Nominatim), which returns the street and town. Your position is also sent to the emergency contact you set, by text message, when you ask. We do not store it.
  • Contacts. Only when you say "set my emergency contact to" and a name, so the app can find that person's number. Your contacts are not uploaded. The one contact you choose is kept on your phone.
  • Text messages. To send your location to your emergency contact. In the Google Play version the message is opened in your messaging app and you tap send. In the version downloaded from our website, it can be sent directly when you ask.

Information we keep

  • If you create an account: your email address, an optional name, and a scrambled (hashed) password. Nothing else. We use them only so you can sign in.
  • On your phone: your settings (voice, language, text size), your emergency contact, and cached voice phrases. These stay on the phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • If you subscribe or give a gift: the payment itself is taken by Paystack on its own page, so we never see your card or bank details. We keep a financial record of the payment: the email you gave, the amount, Paystack's fee, the reference and the time. It is kept against that email (the one you sign in with) and kept after an account is deleted, because it is an accounting record. Paystack sends the receipt to the same email. A monthly payment unlocks your account for that month; a gift unlocks nothing. We also count how many looks and answers each account or device uses each day (under a keyed code, not your name) to apply the same fair daily limit to everyone and to work out running costs; those counts are deleted after 40 days.
  • Server logs: request time, type, result and a short one-way code for your device, used to keep the service running and to stop abuse. Logs never contain photos, questions, answers or passwords.

Services that help us

  • AI services (Anthropic and Google Gemini) receive a photo and your question to produce the description or answer.
  • Paystack processes your subscription or any gift you choose to give (see above).
  • Microsoft Azure speech creates the Nigerian-English voices from the sentences the assistant is about to say.
  • VoiceVerse translates a sentence into Hausa, Yoruba or Igbo when you choose one of those languages.

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest: no email is needed.
  • Delete your account and its data from inside the app: how to delete your account.
  • Remove any permission at any time in your phone's settings; the matching feature simply stops.

Children

VisionMirror is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the contact details in the Contact section of the home page.
