If your browser refuses or warns

Chrome and other browsers are careful with files that install apps. Find what your phone is showing:

"This type of file can harm your device" / "Download anyway?"

This appears for every app that isn't from the Play Store. It does not mean the file is damaged.

  1. Tap Download anyway (or Keep / OK).
  2. If the message disappeared, open Chrome's menu (three dots), choose Downloads, and tap VisionMirror.apk.
"Blocked" / "Dangerous file blocked" / "Can't download securely"
  1. Open Chrome's menu (three dots) → Downloads. Find the blocked file.
  2. Tap the three dots next to it, or tap the file, then choose Keep or Download anyway.
  3. Still blocked? Open Chrome → menu → Settings → Privacy and security → Safe Browsing, and choose Standard protection (not Enhanced) just for this download. Turn Enhanced back on afterwards if you like.
  4. Or try another browser such as Firefox, Samsung Internet or Edge: the same link works in all of them.
"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source"
  1. Tap Settings in that message.
  2. Turn on Allow from this source. (It is listed under the browser you used, for example Chrome.)
  3. Press Back, then tap Install.

You can turn that permission off again afterwards: Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps.

Google Play Protect says "Blocked" or "App not verified"
  1. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
  2. If you only see "Scan app", choose it, then Install anyway after the scan.
  3. If there is no such choice: open the Play Store → your profile picture → Play Protect → the gear icon, and switch off Improve harmful app detection briefly. Install, then switch it back on.
The download keeps failing or stops halfway
  • Check you have about 100 MB of free storage and a steady connection (Wi-Fi is best).
  • Delete the partial file in Downloads and try again.
  • Try another browser.
"App not installed" or "There was a problem parsing the package"
  • The file may not have finished downloading. Delete it and download again.
  • If an older VisionMirror is installed and the new one won't replace it, uninstall the old one first (your settings will reset).
  • Make sure your phone runs Android 8 or newer: Settings → About phone.
It's a work or school phone

Managed phones often block apps from outside the Play Store. Use a personal phone, or ask whoever manages the device.

Check the file is genuine (optional)

Every file has a fingerprint. After downloading, a tool such as a "hash checker" app can show its SHA-256. It should match the checksum shown in the Download box above. If it doesn't, delete the file and download again from this page.

Good to know

  • Camera: to guide you into frame and take the photo. You can switch between the front and back camera.
  • Microphone: so you can ask follow-up questions out loud. Optional: everything also works by touch.
  • Privacy: your photo is analysed, kept for a few minutes only so you can ask questions about it, then deleted. You can delete it instantly. Location and phone details are removed from every photo.
  • Accounts: optional. You can use the app as a guest, or sign up with an email address so it remembers you.
  • Needs internet to describe your photo. Framing guidance works without it.
  • Your settings are saved on your phone: voice, text size, theme and more.
